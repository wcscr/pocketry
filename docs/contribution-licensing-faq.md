# Contribution licensing FAQ

This optional explanation adds no terms to the [CLA](../CLA.md).

## Why ask for additional licensing permission?

AGPL already allows commercial use. The additional grant lets Sugarcreek Research,
LLC offer terms AGPL alone does not permit, including proprietary versions and
reuse in other products. You retain copyright and may license your own work
elsewhere, including to competitors. Neither party gains rights in someone else's
underlying material through your grant.

## Will every contribution be published?

No. The Company promises AGPL licensing for contributions and its published
modifications in Pocketry's public repository. It does not promise to publish
every private submission, security fix, or commercial version, or to host the
repository forever. Existing AGPL grants remain under their terms.

The CLA is not an NDA. You can publish your own work unless separately bound by
agreed confidentiality terms. Other contributors' and dependency licenses still
apply to any private delivery.

## Does this let every user make proprietary versions?

No. The additional copyright grant goes to the Company. Others need an authorized
sublicense to exercise those additional rights; receiving an AGPL copy alone does
not provide them. The limited patent grant is separate.

## What does accepting once mean?

Acceptance covers the identified submission and future intentional contributions.
Other earlier work needs explicit identification. Ordinary reports and user
projects are excluded unless offered for incorporation. New terms or a different
rights holder require appropriate acceptance; maintainers help resolve coverage.

You can accept privately. The [privacy notice](contributor-privacy.md) explains
the records. You owe no ongoing support or litigation assistance. Contact
pocketry@sugarcreekresearch.com with questions about authority or legal capacity.
