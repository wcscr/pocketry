# Bessey Utility Knife

A single-tool Gridfinity bin for the closed Bessey D-BKWH folding utility
knife. Its wood-inlay handle and belt clip fit into a shaped pocket, with a
transverse finger-access opening for lifting the knife.

- **Bin size:** 3 × 6 half-pitch grid (1.5 × 3 full-size cells); 4.5u. The footprint is 62.5 × 125.5 mm, with an overall height of 35.05 mm including the stacking lip.
- **Base:** Gridfinity, with a stacking lip and 69% solid fill height.
- **Pocket depth:** 9.78 mm.
- **Print model:** [bessey-utility-knife.3mf](bessey-utility-knife.3mf).
- **Editable project:** [bessey-utility-knife.pocketry.json](bessey-utility-knife.pocketry.json).
