Pocketry sample projects

Explore editable tool bins with fitted pockets, finger access, and labels. Sample notes show photos of finished prints and explain which variants were printed.

Open a sample in Pocketry

In Bin, choose Library → Sample Library to open a design. To use a downloaded .pocketry.json file, choose Project → Import Project. Open a .3mf in your slicer and choose settings for your printer and filament.

Download the editable library to import all the designs through Library → Import library.
