# Slanted walls prototype

Pocketry can tilt both opposing bin walls or just one along either the X or Y axis while leaving the Gridfinity base flat. This is intended for tilted pockets that need more room near the top of the bin. The selected wall moves by `(height above the 7 mm base) × tan(angle)`. Positive angles move it toward the positive end of the selected axis; negative angles move it the other way. With both walls selected, the body leans uniformly. With one wall selected, the opposite wall stays vertical and the connecting side walls taper between them; the rim stays horizontal. Inward angles that would make the walls meet are rejected.

Enable **App settings → Enable experimental features**, then in **Bin → Construction**, enable **Slanted walls (prototype)**, choose Width (X) or Length (Y), and set the angle. **Walls to tilt** offers Both walls, Left only (−X), or Right only (+X) for X; for Y it offers Front only (−Y) or Back only (+Y). Older designs retain Both walls. With a pocket selected, **Match selected pocket angle** copies its single-axis lean while preserving the selected wall. The 2D layout shows the fixed base footprint with a dashed outline for the rim. The displayed outer dimensions include the body overhang. Turning experimental features off hides the slant controls while preserving saved geometry, previews, exports, and undo/redo history.

This prototype uses a flush rim. Label tabs follow the slanted wall and can be placed on any boundary edge; their top remains at the rim height. On bins below 3u, the tab becomes shallower so its sloped support stays above the fixed base. It rejects a stacking lip, surface text, separate finger access, and a 1u body. Existing pocket depth and tilt controls still apply. The 3D build checks pocket shafts against the actual slanted wall and label tab, and blocks export on a collision. The upper body can extend over adjacent Gridfinity cells, so a slanted bin may depend on neighboring bins or a drawer wall for support. Stacking, drawer clearance, slicer behavior, and physical fit have not been qualified.

The geometry is original to Pocketry. The concept was informed by [cdslug's slanted Gridfinity bin](https://github.com/cdslug/slanted-gridfinity) and the [IKEA Alex example](https://www.reddit.com/r/gridfinity/comments/1x8j3zi/slanted_gridfinity_bin_for_the_ikea_alex_top/).
