# Contour symmetry

Available under **Outline → Symmetry & straighten**, and in the phone’s
**Adjust** controls.

- Drag the two handles to set the centerline; arrow keys make small adjustments.
- Zoom with the buttons, scroll wheel, or pinch. Drag the photo to pan; **Fit** restores the full contour.
- Select **Symmetry** mode, then under **Align with side**, choose **Left**, **Right**, or **Average** to mirror the cleaner side or average both sides, preserving tapers and shoulders.
- **Straighten only** is selected each time the dialog opens. It uses the suggested axis to rotate the photo and all contours without mirroring. Asymmetry, holes, and branched shapes are preserved; the axis handles can still be adjusted.
- In Symmetry mode, **Align upright** also rotates the photo and all contours together. Size and scale stay unchanged.
- Mode and side choices use the same segmented selectors as the Bin controls, with arrow-key navigation. Side choices appear only in Symmetry mode and are retained while switching modes.
- **Reset axis** is beside the preview zoom controls. Contextual guidance is behind help icons, available on hover, focus, or tap.
- Symmetry affects only the selected shape. **Apply** makes one undoable edit; **Cancel** discards the preview.
- Photo alignment survives reloads and re-detection. The original pixels are retained to avoid repeated resampling.

Mirroring either side preserves concave grips and recesses, including edges that
turn back along the centerline. **Average** requires a continuous width
profile; choose one side when the outline branches. Holes and split mirrored
halves are rejected. Shadows can
bias the suggested axis; the user chooses the centerline and cleaner edge.
