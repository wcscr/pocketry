What improved

1.10 recovers dark housings, reflective metal and clear parts that 1.9 often omitted. The DeWalt screwdriver, Craftsman tape measure, calipers, folding knife and Ryobi cutter show substantial gains in complete silhouettes.

What still needs review

The clear needle hub and one long Wiha driver remain fragmented. The tube's white cap is still missed. Shadows and a photography support wire create new protrusions on otherwise smoother outlines. A connected outline is not proof of accuracy or printable fit.

Method, exact versions and limits

1.9.0: 4f6dc95b, immediately before the detection merge. 1.10.0: f6fbccc0, the merged release. This compares shipped defaults, including 1.10's original-photo sampling, Tool edges detection and automatic cleanup.

For each entry and version: open a fresh browser context, choose the untouched original JPEG, accept the offered automatic perspective/scale action, and drag the same tool region. No sensitivity changes, brush corrections, manual simplification, injected calibration or saved outlines were used as inputs. Read-only observations captured the final saved and displayed outlines. Original hashes, corrected-photo bytes, calibration and actual regions match across each pair.
