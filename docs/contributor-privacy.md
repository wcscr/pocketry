# Contribution acceptance privacy notice

Sugarcreek Research, LLC keeps your legal name, account identifiers, agreement and
acceptance text, covered submissions, and relevant organizational/authority details
to administer contributions, document licensing rights, and handle disputes.
Email acceptance also retains the correspondence and reply address.

Public PR comments remain public. Anyone may instead accept privately by emailing
pocketry@sugarcreekresearch.com; maintainers use a record reference in the PR
without publishing private details. Private records have restricted access and
may be shared as needed with legal advisers, a successor assuming the agreement,
or as required by law.

Records are kept while reasonably needed to document grants being relied on or
meet legal obligations, with periodic review to remove unnecessary data. Contact
pocketry@sugarcreekresearch.com to correct records or ask about access or deletion;
legally necessary evidence may need to be retained. Do not send home addresses,
identity documents, or other unnecessary personal information.
