# Gerber Multitool

A single-tool Gridfinity bin for the closed Gerber Multitool shown in the
combined-bin photo. A shaped pocket follows its folded handles, with a
transverse finger-access opening for lifting the tool.

- **Bin size:** 3 × 6 half-pitch grid (1.5 × 3 full-size cells); 4.5u. The footprint is 62.5 × 125.5 mm, with an overall height of 35.05 mm including the stacking lip.
- **Base:** Gridfinity, with a stacking lip and 69% solid fill height.
- **Pocket depth:** 16.28 mm.
- **Print model:** [gerber-multitool.3mf](gerber-multitool.3mf).
- **Editable project:** [gerber-multitool.pocketry.json](gerber-multitool.pocketry.json).

## Layout

<a href="layout.svg"><img src="layout.svg" width="250" alt="Single Gerber Multitool bin layout with a shaped pocket and transverse finger access"></a>

This single-tool layout uses the Gerber outline from the
[combined Bessey and Gerber bin](../bessey-gerber/). Gerber models vary in
size and shape; compare the pocket with your tool and print a fit check before
the full bin. The 3MF includes separate body, pocket-floor, and rim color parts;
use your own print settings at **100% scale**.
