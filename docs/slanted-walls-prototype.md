# Slanted walls prototype

Pocketry can lean the whole bin body along either the X or Y axis while leaving the Gridfinity base flat. This is intended for tilted pockets that need more room near the top of the bin. The body is sheared continuously from the top of the 7 mm base to the flush rim: each horizontal cross-section keeps its shape, and its offset is `(height above base) × tan(angle)`. Positive angles move the rim toward the positive end of the selected axis; negative angles move it the other way.

Enable **App settings → Enable experimental features**, then in **Bin → Construction**, enable **Slanted walls (prototype)**, choose Width (X) or Length (Y), and set the angle. With a pocket selected, **Match selected pocket angle** copies its single-axis lean when that pocket's direction is within 1° of X or Y. The 2D layout shows the fixed base footprint with a dashed outline for the shifted rim. The displayed outer dimensions include the body overhang. Turning experimental features off hides the slant controls while preserving saved geometry, previews, exports, and undo/redo history.

This prototype uses a flush rim. Label tabs follow the slanted wall and can be placed on any boundary edge; their top remains at the rim height. On bins below 3u, the tab becomes shallower so its sloped support stays above the fixed base. It rejects a stacking lip, surface text, separate finger access, and a 1u body. Existing pocket depth and tilt controls still apply. The 3D build checks pocket shafts against the actual slanted wall and label tab, and blocks export on a collision. The upper body can extend over adjacent Gridfinity cells, so a slanted bin may depend on neighboring bins or a drawer wall for support. Stacking, drawer clearance, slicer behavior, and physical fit have not been qualified.

The geometry is original to Pocketry. The concept was informed by [cdslug's slanted Gridfinity bin](https://github.com/cdslug/slanted-gridfinity) and the [IKEA Alex example](https://www.reddit.com/r/gridfinity/comments/1x8j3zi/slanted_gridfinity_bin_for_the_ikea_alex_top/).
