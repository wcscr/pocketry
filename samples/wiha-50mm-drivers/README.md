# Wiha 50 mm drivers

A pair of Wiha precision screwdrivers with 50 mm blades: a **260 / 3.0 × 50
slotted driver** and a **261 / PH0 × 50 Phillips driver**. Linked shaped pockets
and shared finger access hold both drivers in the same direction.

- **Blade length:** 50 mm.
- **Bin size:** 1 × 4 grid; 3u. The footprint is 41.5 × 167.5 mm, with an overall height of 24.55 mm including the stacking lip.
- **Base:** Gridfinity, with a stacking lip and 80% solid fill height.
- **Print model:** [wiha-50mm-drivers.3mf](wiha-50mm-drivers.3mf).
- **Editable project:** [wiha-50mm-drivers.pocketry.json](wiha-50mm-drivers.pocketry.json).

[How to open, edit, and print](../README.md#using-a-sample) ·
[Complete editable sample library](../pocketry-sample-library.json).

## Fit and stacking

The drivers sit slightly too high to stack the loaded bins without some
interference. If stacking is desired, lower the driver pockets by about **1 mm**
in the editable project before exporting a revised print model.
